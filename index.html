See the calm

Spend one minute with your pet. Feel the difference.

Watching a companion relax — even on screen — is proven to soothe the nervous system. Here's a freely-licensed clip we love, plus the science of why it works.

  • Lowers blood pressure — a 10-minute petting session has real, measurable calming effects.
  • Releases oxytocin — the same hormone that bonds mother and baby, now flowing between you and your pet.
  • Breaks isolation — consistent routine and companionship is powerful medicine for loneliness.
