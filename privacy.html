Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Your trust matters — here's exactly what we do with your information.

What we collect

  • Contact form & newsletter: your name and email address, if you choose to provide them.
  • Reminder schedule: the pet care schedule you build is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never sent to us.

How we use it

We use contact details only to respond to you and, if you subscribed, to send the updates you asked for. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Cookies & local storage

We use local storage on your device to remember your reminder schedule and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies. If we add analytics or payment providers later, we'll update this page and only use reputable, consent-compliant vendors.

Third parties

If you complete a subscription, payment is processed by a trusted third-party payment provider and your card details are never stored on our servers. We may use a form service to receive contact messages.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by emailing hello@pawcompanion.example. You can clear your locally stored reminders in your browser settings.

Children's privacy

Our service is intended for adult pet owners. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
