Caring for a Senior Pet: The Gentle Guide

PawCompanion Editorial · Pet-care content team· Updated 2026

Watching a companion grow older is bittersweet. With a few gentle adjustments, the senior years can still be full of comfort, closeness and joy — for both of you.

In this guide
  1. Food that fits
  2. Movement, softened
  3. Comfort first
  4. The gentle check-in

Food that fits

Older pets need easier-to-digest food, smaller more frequent meals, and plenty of fresh water. Keep meals at consistent times — predictability is pure comfort for an ageing body.

Movement, softened

Swap long hikes for several short, slow strolls with plenty of sniffing. On slippery floors, add grip — a yoga mat or non-slip socks can prevent the panicky scramble that leads to injury.

Comfort first

  • Soft, warm bedding in a draft-free corner.
  • Raised bowls to spare stiff joints.
  • Gentle grooming that also lets you check the skin and pads.
Golden rule for seniors: little and often. Small meals, short walks, frequent rest.

The gentle check-in

Track appetite, mood, energy and bathroom habits daily. Small, consistent observations catch changes early — before they become bigger problems.
